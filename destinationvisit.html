BUDDHIST HERITAGE :- Explore the path of Buddha

Wanderwise also encourages students to explore important buddhist heritage sites. these places provide an opportunity to learn about Buddhism, ancient indian culture and the life of GAUTAMA BUDDHA

GAUTAMA BUDDHA make people follow the path of wisdom , his places can provide a quiet envirnoment for reflection and peace

  • Bodh Gaya - Place associated with Buddha's enlightenment
  • Sarnath - Place associated with Buddha's first teaching
  • Kushinagar - Place associated with Budhha's final passing

HIMACHAL PRADESH -Beautiful Valleys

Himachal pradesh is wonderful destination for student who love mountains, valleys, rivers and peaceful landscapes. Places such as Manali, Dharamshala and Spiyi valley offer beautiful scenery and memorable experiences

  • Manali - Mountains and valleys
  • Dharamshala - Mountains and Tibetan culture
  • Spiti Valley - High-altitude Landscapes

NORTHEAST INDIA - Mountains, Valleys and Nature

Northeast india is known for its beautiful mountains, valleys, forests, waterfalls and unique culture . It is an excellent choice for students who want to experience peaceful landscapes and explore less crowded destination

  • Meghalaya - Waterfalls, Caves and green valleys
  • Sikkim - Mountains and beautiful landscapes
  • Arunachal pradesh - Valleys, Forest and Mountains

KERALA - God's Own Country

Kerala is famous for its beautiful Backwaters, Beaches, Green Hills, Waterfalls and Rich Culture . Students can enjoy nature, local foods and peaceful surroundings while exploring places like Munnar, Alleppey and Varkala

  • Munnar - Beautiful hills and tea gardens
  • Alleppey - Famous Backwater
  • Varkala - Beautiful beach and cliffs

NEPAL- Mountains and Heritages

Nepal is a beautiful country known for the Himalayas, ancient temples, beautiful cities and rich cultural heritage. Students can explore places such as Kathmandu, Pokhara and Lumbini while experiencing a different culture

  • Kathmandu - Temples and Culturals heritage
  • Pokhara - Lakes and Mountain views
  • Lumbini - Important Buddhist heritage sites
