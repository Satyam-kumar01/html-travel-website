FREE TRAVEL OPPORTUNITY

Wanderwise believes that talented and hardworking students deserve special opportunities. We offer selected students a chance to travel for free, where their travel expenses are covered by Wanderwise.

ELIGIBILITY

Students can apply for the free travel opportunity if they have good academic performance or have achieved something special in their field of talent.

  • Good academic performance
  • Achievements in sports, arts or other activities
  • Participation or achievement in competitions
  • Special talent or skills

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

Interested students should send the following documents to ourofficial Wanderwise email:

  • Academic mark sheets or certificates
  • Achievement certificates, if available
  • Proof of talent or competition participation
  • Student ID card
  • Short introduction about yourself

HOW TO APPLY?

Send all the required documents along with your name, contact details and a short description of your achievements to our Wanderwise email.

Email: wanderwise12@gmail.com

After reviewing the applications, selected students will be contacted by Wanderwise.
