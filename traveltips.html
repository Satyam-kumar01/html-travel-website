WANDERWISE TRAVEL TIPS

Travel Safely, Travel Responsibly, Travel Happily

🌍 TRAVEL WITH WANDERWISE

At Wanderwise, we are always there to support you during your journey. Your safety, comfort and happiness are important to us. But a great journey also depends on how responsibly we behave.

So, while we take care of your travel needs, we request you to follow some simple rules and travel with respect, kindness and responsibility.

🛡️ STAY SAFE

  • Always keep your important documents and belongings safe.
  • Follow the safety instructions given by Wanderwise.
  • Stay with your group and avoid going to unsafe places alone.
  • Keep emergency contact numbers with you.
  • Inform your travel coordinator if you face any problem.

BE KIND AND RESPECTFUL

  • Respect local people and their culture.
  • Respect different religions, traditions and lifestyles.
  • Be polite and helpful to other travelers.
  • Follow the rules of the places you visit.

PROTECT NATURE

  • Do not throw garbage in public places.
  • Keep mountains, rivers, beaches and forests clean.
  • Do not damage plants, animals or historical places.
  • Use natural resources responsibly.

BE A RESPONSIBLE TRAVELER

Travelling gives us the opportunity to see new places and meet new people. We should make sure that our presence does not create problems for others or harm the environment.

Be moral. Be respectful. Be responsible. Leave every place a little better than you found it.

✨ ENJOY YOUR JOURNEY

Explore new places, learn new things, make beautiful memories and enjoy every moment of your journey.
